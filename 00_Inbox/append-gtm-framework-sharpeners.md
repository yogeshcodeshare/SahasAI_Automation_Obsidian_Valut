## Revision 2026-08-06 — framework sharpeners + coaching-class niche validated

Refines (does not replace) Phases 1–5 above, after reviewing four AI-agency positioning videos (Samin Yasar) and pressure-testing the first vertical.

**Niche confirmed (Phase 3).** "Coaching classes" is a sound first vertical for our model. Key distinction the videos blur: their "coaching" = US high-ticket online coaches (setter-replacement AI agents, %-of-revenue pricing, 25+ employees); **ours = Indian offline coaching classes / tuition institutes** (customers are parents+students, one admission ₹25k–1L). Use the videos' niche-selection and positioning logic; **ignore their product and pricing model.** Detail in [[coaching-class-vertical-playbook]].

**Add a CATEGORY layer, not just a niche (sharpens Phase 3).** "AI for coaching classes" is still generic. Own an outcome-named category — **the Admissions Machine for Karad coaching classes** (the package name already in [[pricing-ladder]] / [[coaching-class-vertical-playbook]]). Use that language everywhere (website, pitch, GBP) and refuse feature-vs-feature comparisons.

**Proof-capture becomes an explicit SOP (strengthens Phase 2 + 4).** For every client incl. the Manovedh pilot: screenshot BEFORE metrics on day one (GBP views/calls/directions, review count, owner reply-rate, median first-response time), track through, document AFTER in hard numbers; organise case studies by vertical. Target: undeniable proof within ~90 days. Highest-leverage habit — do it from client #1.

**Manual-first → document → productise loop (makes Phase 2 deliberate).** Do the work by hand first, log every step/objection/what-converted, then let the repeatable parts harden into the [[gmb-sarathi]] + WhatsApp SOPs. "You can't automate what you don't yet understand."

**Sequencing within a coaching client.** Lead with the free audit/demo (Trojan Horse) → fix the single most measurable bottleneck first → expand into the everyday parent automations (fee reminders, attendance, results) as the retainer, per [[coaching-class-vertical-playbook]].

**Guardrails unchanged.** Pricing stays `emerging` — validate on the first 3 paid calls ([[pricing-ladder]]); while DataForSEO is deferred use demo-labelled audits only (no live-data or ranking promises); scale trigger stays **5–8 stable clients + written portability terms** ([[bizautomation-reseller-deal]]).
