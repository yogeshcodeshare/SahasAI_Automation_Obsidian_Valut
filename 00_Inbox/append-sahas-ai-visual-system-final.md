## Finalized visual system (2026-08-14)

Locked brand palette and typography for Sahas AI (agency brand — website, collateral, client-facing PDFs). **This is the AGENCY brand and is distinct from the GMB Sarathi tool UI** (which uses its own green/marigold system).

### Palette (exact hex)
| Role | Hex | Name | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | `#1B1714` | deep charcoal | primary text; dark section backgrounds; logo on light |
| Accent | `#D9A441` | muted gold | CTAs, buttons, highlights, the "a✦" mark — the 10%, never a flood |
| Secondary | `#F5F1E8` | warm cream | cards, alternating sections, letterhead |
| Background | `#FAF8F3` | soft background | default page background |
| Supporting | `#746A60` | warm grey | secondary/supporting text, captions, muted labels |

Proportion: **60% soft-bg/cream · 30% charcoal · 10% gold** (gold signals, never floods).

### Typography
| Use | Font |
|---|---|
| Hero headlines, section titles | **DM Serif Display** |
| Body paragraphs | **Manrope Regular (400)** |
| Navigation, buttons, labels | **Manrope 600** |
| Small captions | **Manrope 500** |

Both are Google Fonts (free). DM Serif Display carries the premium/editorial feel; Manrope keeps UI clean and readable.

### Logo
Interlocking-loops monogram + wordmark "SAHAS a✦" (SAHAS in DM Serif Display caps, "ai" in gold with the ✦ spark). Vector master: `Sahas_AI_Production_Master_Tight.svg`. Variants + mockups per [[free-brand-kit-funnel-plan]].
