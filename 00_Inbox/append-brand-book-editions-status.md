## Brand book editions (2026-08-25)

The Sahas AI brand-guidelines **book** now exists as a real deliverable, generated in **Claude Design** from the [[brand-book-kit-mega-prompt]] fed with [[brand-imprint-sahas-ai]] content and the finalized [[append-sahas-ai-visual-system-final|visual system]]. Three editions are being produced, all sharing the same verbatim copy, the real logo files (`Sahas_AI_Production_Master_Tight.svg` + PNGs), and the same honest-evidence guardrails.

- **Light Edition (v1.0) — DONE, canonical.** 22 pages, DM Serif Display + Manrope, real logo embedded on every placement, exact locked palette. Reviewed page-by-page against the mega-prompt: fully compliant — all copy verbatim, no fabricated proof, "Google/Meta Partner" correctly absent, "service brand of Chitrartha Enterprises" on the copyright line. One minor fix requested: use the transparent reversed logo on dark surfaces (business card front / email signature) so no light box sits behind the mark. This is the reference example for the planned "Free Business Brand Kit" website funnel ([[free-brand-kit-funnel-plan]]).
- **Dark Edition — in progress.** Charcoal-first, cream text, gold accent, inverted cream divider pages, a fresh (non-DM-Serif/Manrope) font pairing chosen by the tool. Same structure + copy.
- **New Era Edition — in progress.** Gen-Z-fluent new-age AI-startup look balanced to stay credible to a business owner (Linear/Vercel/Framer restraint + warm Indian-startup optimism): bigger scale contrast, oversized chapter numbers, the gold spark reused as a graphic accent, optional single cool secondary accent in tiny doses, a fresh grotesk + optional monospace pairing. Built by merging the New-Era design direction INTO the full [[brand-book-kit-mega-prompt]] so it inherits every structural + guardrail rule.

**Reusable takeaway:** the mega-prompt now has two proven style variants layered on top of it — a **Dark-mode** direction and a **New-Era/Gen-Z** direction — that can be reused per customer in the Free Brand Kit funnel by swapping only the design-direction block while keeping the master structure and guardrails intact.
