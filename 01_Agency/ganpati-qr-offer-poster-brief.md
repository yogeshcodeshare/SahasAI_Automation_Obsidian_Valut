# Ganpati QR Offer Poster Brief

## Campaign use

Prepare an original Sahas AI QR-code offer campaign for publishing from 2026-09-14 onward, using the supplied festive QR-code posters only as visual references. This brief does not copy any third-party brand, logo, phone number, address, exact wording or exact layout.

## Reusable generation prompt

Create a clean, trustworthy, Marathi-first promotional poster for **Sahas AI** and a Ganpati Utsav QR-code offer for local businesses. Use a warm festive Indian visual direction with restrained Ganpati-season cues, green/ saffron/ cream accents, strong contrast, generous spacing and a modern digital-business feel. Keep the Sahas AI logo clear and separate from the main artwork.

Headline: **या गणेशोत्सवात तुमच्या व्यवसायाला द्या स्मार्ट डिजिटल ओळख**

Supporting line: **Reviews, payments, website आणि social media साठी custom QR codes**

Show a neat set of clearly labelled service cards:

- Google Review QR
- UPI / Payment QR
- Website QR
- Social Media QR
- vCard / Contact QR
- Location, Menu किंवा Wi-Fi QR

Use a simple QR-code visual with a visible quiet zone, but do not present a decorative placeholder as a real scannable customer QR. Add a clear CTA: **तुमच्या व्यवसायासाठी QR कोड तयार करून घ्या**. Use these placeholders until the founder approves the final commercial details: **[STARTING PRICE]**, **[SAHAS WHATSAPP CTA]**, **[SAHAS WEBSITE]**, and **[SAHAS AI LOGO]**.

Do not add claims such as lifetime validity, unlimited scans, 24/7 support, guaranteed satisfaction or guaranteed review growth unless Sahas AI has actually confirmed that it can deliver them. Do not use tiny text, fake testimonials, invented addresses or unapproved contact details. Make all important Marathi text readable and proofread the Devanagari before export.

## Export and QA

- Primary social version: 1080 × 1350 portrait for Instagram and Facebook.
- Secondary version: 1080 × 1080 square.
- Optional print version: A4 with a high-resolution export.
- Keep the offer, CTA and contact action in the visual safe area.
- Test every real QR code after export on multiple phones and under ordinary lighting.
- Produce an accessible text/caption version alongside the image.
- Human approval is required before publishing or paid promotion.

## Reference boundary

The supplied examples demonstrate useful hierarchy: festive header, service list, QR-card examples, benefit strip and a strong CTA. They are inspiration only. The final Sahas asset must use Sahas-approved copy, identity, pricing and contact details and must remain distinct from the reference designs.
