# Sahas AI Founder Blueprint — Final Direction as of 2026-09-10

Yogesh confirmed that the decisions in the Founder Blueprint conversation are the current Sahas AI direction. When an older vault note conflicts with this record, this dated record takes precedence. Preserve the older note as history, mark it superseded or partially superseded, and link it here.

## Six-month commitment

For six months, Sahas AI is the primary business-building focus. The execution sequence is build, demonstrate, publish, pitch, collect feedback, improve, and sell. New business ideas, unrelated courses, Google Ads, and Google Business Profile work remain in the Future Ideas parking lot unless Yogesh explicitly reactivates one through a newer decision.

Decision filter: **Will this directly help Sahas AI get its first paying customers?**

If yes, schedule and execute it. If no, park it.

## Current market position — Pan-India, multi-industry

Sahas AI is a Pan-India business-automation agency. It is not positioned as a Karad-only, Maharashtra-only, or generic "local business" agency. Maharashtra remains a useful first research and relationship market, and Marathi remains a useful content language, but neither limits delivery geography.

Sahas AI will work across selected industries inside its automation niche. Industry priority is determined through the Michele Torti five-criteria system and India-specific evidence, not founder preference alone.

The five criteria are:

1. Meaningful customer ticket value and economic room to pay for automation.
2. Obvious, recurring operational pain.
3. Reachable owner or commercial decision-maker.
4. A result that can be measured within a bounded pilot.
5. Delivery Sahas AI can implement with controlled complexity and human approval.

Current portfolio for sequential research and validation:

- S1 — Modular kitchen and interior-design studios.
- S2 — Complete home-renovation contractors.
- S3 — Wedding venues and banquet halls.
- S4 — Solar EPC and rooftop installers.
- A1 — Coaching and job-training institutes.
- A2 — Recruitment and staffing agencies.
- A3 — Aesthetic, dermatology and premium wellness clinics.
- A4 — Dental, diagnostic and physiotherapy clinics.
- A5 — B2B logistics providers.
- A6 — Travel agents, tour operators and boutique hotels.
- A7 — Multi-technician home-service AMC and installation firms.
- A8 — Premium car dealerships and multi-brand service centres.
- B1 — B2B machinery suppliers and industrial distributors.
- B2 — Real-estate brokers and developers.
- B3 — CA, accounting and consulting firms.
- B4 — Legal firms.
- B5 — Commercial insurance brokers and agency principals.

These rankings are research hypotheses until supported by interviews, workflow evidence, willingness to pay and pilot results. S-tier means research and outreach priority; it does not guarantee an easy sale.

Coaching classes are no longer the default first vertical or Sahas AI's defining niche. Coaching and job-training institutes remain one A-tier category that may be served if it passes the same evidence gates as every other industry.

Each category must be developed sequentially through a subtype and buyer map, Indian price evidence, pains separated by workflow stage, pain intensity, pain-matched solution design, human handoff and safety boundaries, a measurable pilot, and founder approval before its chapter becomes final.

## GoHighLevel decision

GHL is part of the active founder blueprint because the account is already included in the paid Biz Automation package and directly supports Sahas AI lead management and sales execution.

The bounded GHL scope is:

1. Business profile and agency identity.
2. Contact structure and required fields.
3. Lead pipeline and stage ownership.
4. Forms and lead capture.
5. Calendar only where it improves booking or handoff.
6. Facebook, Instagram, and other relevant integrations.
7. Necessary automations, tasks, alerts, and reporting.

This is not authorization to learn every GHL feature. Build only the parts required by Sahas AI's own funnel and first client pilots.

This supersedes the original indefinite GHL deferral in [[decision-ghl-deferral]]. It refines the August update by making GHL a bounded, parallel business-setup workstream during the first eight weeks, rather than requiring all WhatsApp and n8n work to be completed before any GHL setup begins.

## First eight-week outcome

By the end of week eight, Sahas AI should have:

- Three to five strong demonstrations across two or three selected industries.
- A working internal lead path and pipeline.
- A bounded GHL setup connected to relevant channels.
- Demonstrated WhatsApp workflows based on Biz Automation LMS learning.
- At least one n8n or AI-agent workflow with clear human handoff.
- A credible, conversion-focused SahasAI.in website.
- Approximately three useful founder-led short videos per week documenting the build.
- A repeatable outreach, discovery, feedback, proposal, and follow-up process.
- Real sales conversations and a focused attempt to win the first paying customers.

## Eight-week operating sequence

### Week 1 — foundation and account ownership

- Confirm the paid-package components, login ownership, recovery access, and support boundaries.
- Create or standardize the Sahas AI Instagram and YouTube accounts.
- Complete consistent brand names, descriptions, contact routes, and social links.
- Configure the GHL business profile and basic user/account settings.
- Define target industries and the first three demonstration problems.
- Audit SahasAI.in for messaging, services, trust, CTA, mobile behavior, and missing business information.
- Publish three founder-led videos around the problem Sahas AI solves and the build journey.

### Week 2 — internal lead path

- Build the contact structure, mandatory fields, lead sources, pipeline stages, next-action field, and named owner in GHL.
- Connect the existing Facebook Page and new Instagram account where technically supported and appropriate.
- Create one basic lead form or WhatsApp entry route.
- Build the first enquiry acknowledgement, qualification, and owner-alert workflow using approved messages.
- Implement the highest-impact website fixes: clear positioning, services, CTA, WhatsApp capture, social links, and contact/business information.
- Publish three videos showing the missed-enquiry problem, the safe three-question capture, and a sample handoff.

### Week 3 — first vertical demonstration

- Build one complete enquiry-to-handoff demonstration for the highest-priority initial vertical.
- Add survey, consultation, or appointment logic only if the chosen problem requires it.
- Define test cases, exception cases, human approval points, and proof screenshots.
- Start a small prospect list and record evidence for each prospect.
- Publish three videos explaining the workflow, its human handoff, and the problem it prevents.

### Week 4 — second demonstration and outreach start

- Build a second demonstration for another recurring business problem or industry.
- Add follow-up tasks, next-action dates, and stale-lead visibility.
- Create a short discovery call structure and demo walkthrough.
- Begin personalized outreach to a small qualified group.
- Record objections and buyer language; revise the offer based on evidence.
- Publish three useful videos tied to the second demonstration.

### Week 5 — bounded AI agent

- Build one tightly scoped AI assistant or agent using internship learning.
- Restrict it to approved knowledge, non-sensitive tasks, and clear human escalation.
- Add logs, failure alerts, and a manual review point.
- Improve the website demonstration/proof section without delaying sales work.
- Continue outreach, follow-up, and discovery conversations.
- Publish three videos explaining what the agent can do, cannot do, and when it hands over.

### Week 6 — pilot readiness

- Select the strongest three demonstrations and remove weak or duplicate ones.
- Package a small paid pilot with explicit scope, timeline, client inputs, exclusions, and success metric.
- Prepare proposal, onboarding checklist, and acceptance criteria.
- Review GHL reporting and pipeline hygiene.
- Ask qualified prospects for a paid pilot.
- Publish three videos about buying automation, pilot scope, and measurable outcomes.

### Week 7 — objections, trust, and delivery refinement

- Use prospect feedback to improve the demonstrations and proposal.
- Build opt-out, exception, escalation, and failure-recovery behavior.
- Add credible trust material to the website; do not fabricate testimonials, results, or client claims.
- Continue outreach and close-follow-up activity.
- Publish three videos about permission, responsible follow-up, human review, and lessons from feedback.

### Week 8 — evidence and next-quarter decision

- Review demos, content, sales activity, pipeline, and prospect feedback.
- Document what produced replies, booked conversations, proposals, and rejections.
- Select the strongest vertical and offer for the next 60–90 days.
- Archive or park weak workstreams.
- Set the next milestone based on actual evidence and paying-customer progress.
- Publish three videos showing what was built, what was learned, and the next workflow to test.

## Website workstream boundary

SahasAI.in supports credibility and conversion, so it remains active but time-boxed. Work includes:

- Messaging and positioning audit.
- Clear service and automation-demo sections.
- Primary CTA and WhatsApp lead capture.
- Trust and credibility elements that can be supported.
- Social links and contact, legal, and business information approved for publication.
- Mobile performance and usability.
- Basic SEO and technical hygiene.

Website polishing must not delay demonstrations, outreach, or sales conversations. Use one or two bounded sessions per week after the initial high-impact fixes.

## Learning boundary

The Ankit Ravindra Jain paid portal is a supporting sales resource. Use only modules tied to the current sales task: sales psychology, consultative selling, communication, elevator pitch, persuasion, follow-up, proposal discussion, and objection handling. It is not a second full-time curriculum.

Michele Torti's journey and agency material are a mentor reference for niche selection, problem discovery, offer design, manual validation, proof, outreach, and productization. Adapt the principles to Indian and Maharashtrian clients. Do not copy claims, scripts, prices, or market assumptions without local evidence.

Video editing is a minimum viable skill, not a 41-hour learning project during the first eight weeks. Yogesh needs enough editing to trim, add readable captions, improve audio, add a simple hook/title, insert a few proof visuals, and export vertical video. Advanced Premiere Pro, After Effects, VFX, motion graphics, and cinematic polishing remain parked unless content performance creates a clear need.

- Begin Marathi-first for Maharashtra local-business owners, using natural English business and automation terms where they are commonly understood.
- Keep one neutral Sahas AI account while testing some Hinglish content.
- Do not split Marathi and Hinglish into separate channels before publishing enough content to show a real audience or positioning conflict.
- Target approximately three short videos per week.
- Content documents real problems, demonstrations, lessons, buyer questions, and honest build progress.
- Avoid unverified performance claims and generic AI-news posting.
