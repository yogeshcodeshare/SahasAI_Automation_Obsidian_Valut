# WhatsApp Lead SOP — 8-Stage Automation

The lead spine (from the real-estate training case). Works for any business — only the questions and the offer change. Backbone of the WhatsApp offer.

## The 8 stages
1. **Lead In** — ad / QR / website / Google → auto-greeting (free, user-initiated)
2. **Requirement Capture** — bot asks qualifying questions → [[crm-and-field-model]]
3. **Showcase / Offer** — send the tailored deliverable
4. **Appointment** — book demo / site visit / call (WhatsApp Flow)
5. **Follow-Up** — nudge at every stage, stop on reply
6. **Conversion / Closing** — close · pay link · onboard
7. **Lost** — capture the reason → win-back list
8. **Review** — Google review + referral

## Supporting pillars
- **Content & Social:** Idea → Content → Creative → Schedule → Post → Track (feeds Stage 1)
- **Customer Management:** Upsell · Cross-sell · Reputation · Relationship (retainer revenue)
- **Channels:** Call · WhatsApp · Email · SMS · IVR (WhatsApp primary)
