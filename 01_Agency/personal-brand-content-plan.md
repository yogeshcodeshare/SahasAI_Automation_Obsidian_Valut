# Personal Brand Content Plan

Yogesh plans to build a personal brand on short-form video first, and use it to bring clients to Sahas AI. This is a plan agreed in chat, not yet tested with an audience.

## Account roles
- **Personal (face, reach, trust):** YouTube *Yogesh Mohite | AI Automation* `@yogeshmohiteai`; Instagram `@yogeshmohite.ai` planned. See [[sahas-ai-social-channel-status]].
- **Agency (demos, offers, client DMs):** Instagram `@sahas_ai_automation`.
- **Language:** Marathi only for the first 3–6 months, with English tech words ([[sahas-content-language-conventions]]). Hindi later on a **separate** account; English mainly on LinkedIn under Yogesh's real name.

## Content mix (a planning lens, not a quota)
| Share | Pillar |
|---|---|
| 40% | Own builds shown solving a business problem — only tested, approved-to-show builds |
| 30% | **"Fake की Real?"** signature series: test viral "comment for the file" automations, show what breaks; criticise the workflow, blur the creator |
| 20% | AI news, only when tied to a business problem |
| 10% | GitHub repo reviews (mostly skipped — they attract developers, not clients) |

## Funnel
- Call to action: comment **DEMO** → automated DM asks business type and pain → WhatsApp intake → 10-minute Business Growth Check ([[decision-marathi-local-business-lead-campaign-direction]]).
- Do not give away workflow JSON files for comments.

## First three reels (scripts drafted 2026-11-11, not yet approved or recorded)
1. **Who I am** — hook: three months ago I didn't know what n8n was.
2. **What I build** — hook: a customer's WhatsApp at 11 PM answered next morning.
3. **"Fake की Real?" launch** — hook: comment for the file… but does it actually work?

Cadence follows [[sahas-ai-reel-production-plan]]: three reels a week, recorded at weekends.
