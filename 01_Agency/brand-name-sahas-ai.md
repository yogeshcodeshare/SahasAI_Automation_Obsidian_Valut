# Brand Name Decision — Sahas AI

Final agency brand: **Sahas AI** (साहस = courage/boldness). Registered as the Udyam enterprise name and used for the Meta Business portfolio ("Sahas AI Business Automation Agency") and WhatsApp setup.

## Why (and what was rejected)
The agency's whole product is "make businesses rank/findable on Google," so the name MUST be ownable — a coined/uncommon word, not a common one. Every meaningful common word or famous soundalike was already taken:
- ❌ **Sarathi** — collides with sarathi.parivahan.gov.in (national driving-licence portal)
- ❌ **Saksham** — NGOs + govt schemes
- ❌ **Paramarsh** — consultancies + UGC scheme
- ❌ **Alexa/Arexa** — Amazon trademark + soundalike; arexa.in domain taken
- ❌ **Adastra** — a major global AI/data consultancy
- ❌ **Dhruvtara / Dhruva Marketing / Dhruva Branding** — taken (Pune/Karnataka)
- ❌ **Botora / Botwala** — taken AI-automation agencies
- ✅ Clean coined finalists explored: Dhruvora, DhruvReach, Tejora, Yashora, Dhruvix
- ✅ **Sahas AI** — chosen (clean brand, founder liked the logo with the ✦ star on "ai")

## Notes
- "Sahas" is a "vibe" name (courage/bold), not a "what-you-do" name → lean on tagline (e.g., *धाडसाने वाढा* / grow boldly).
- "GMB Sarathi" remains the internal TOOL name (nobody Googles the tool); the agency brand is separate → no conflict.
- Domain: aim for `sahas.ai` (premium, on-brand) or `sahasai.in`; `sahasai.com` appeared parked. Business name ≠ domain — Udyam/Veblika did not need the domain first.
- Trademark: GST/Udyam name gives NO brand protection — only a Trade Marks Act registration (Class 35 + 42) does.
