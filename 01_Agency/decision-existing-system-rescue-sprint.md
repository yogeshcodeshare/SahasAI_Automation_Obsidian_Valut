# Decision — Existing-System Rescue Sprint Pilot

## Decision

Test an ethical, fixed-scope **Existing-System Rescue Sprint** for businesses that already own a business-automation or marketing system but are not getting useful workflows from it. Sahas AI first understands the business and the tools already available, then configures and tests practical automations **inside the client's existing system** wherever its documented capability permits.

The pilot service price is **₹5,000 total**, paid against accepted milestones:

| Milestone | Client receives | Fee |
|---|---|---:|
| M1 — discovery and rescue plan accepted | tool/access audit, prioritised workflow plan, written scope | ₹1,000 |
| M2 — first two automations accepted in UAT | two live, testable workflows with handover | ₹2,000 |
| M3 — delivery accepted | up to two further live, testable workflows and final handover | ₹2,000 |

The standard promise is **up to four independent automations**, not "four or five" without definition. A fifth item is included only when it is a small linked action that does not require new data, content, integrations, or testing cycles. This protects both the client and Sahas AI from scope creep.

## Context

Public review pages and LinkedIn posts currently contain customer allegations of missing onboarding, delayed delivery, weak communication, and unusable automation/WhatsApp features from MBG Card. This is a market signal that some business owners may own software but still need practical implementation help. It is **not** proof that every MBG customer has had this experience, and Sahas AI must not repeat allegations as fact or target people because they posted a complaint.

The opportunity is to sell implementation clarity: no automatic demand for a new subscription, small verified milestones, client-owned accounts, documented scope, and a usable handover. The first few engagements are learning/proof pilots, not evidence that the ₹5,000 price is sustainable or that the offer is repeatable.

## Client-Facing Positioning

**Primary hook (recommended):**

> **Software घेतलंय, पण enquiry ला उत्तर आणि follow-up अजून हाताने? नवीन software घेण्याआधी, तुमचं चालू system कामाला लावूया.**

Supporting trust proof:

> आधी तुमचा business आणि system पाहू. काय चालतं, काय थांबलंय, आणि कोणते 4 workflows शक्य आहेत ते लिखित देऊ. मग milestone नुसारच पुढे जाऊ.

The value is local, transparent, and cost-conscious implementation—not a claim that Sahas AI can repair every vendor product or guarantee more customers.

## Scope and Non-Negotiables

- Client owns their accounts, WhatsApp number, Meta assets, and data. Sahas AI uses official collaborator/partner access where supported; never client passwords.
- No new software is required **unless** the current system cannot technically perform a required automation. Any new paid tool, Meta conversation charge, ad spend, or vendor fee is disclosed and approved separately.
- Each automation needs a written trigger, action, owner, success test, and stop/failure behaviour. Examples: enquiry acknowledgement, qualification capture, booking reminder, stale-lead follow-up, payment reminder.
- A live acceptance test and short client handover are required for every paid milestone.
- This is implementation help, not a refund, legal, complaint-resolution, SEO-ranking, sales-growth, or vendor-support guarantee.
- Do not use scraped reviewer data, private phone numbers, or complaint language in outreach. If a business voluntarily asks for help, or is reached through its official public business contact channel, pitch the neutral rescue service without naming or criticising any vendor.

## Alternatives Rejected

- **"We will fix MBG Card" campaign** — rejected: unverified claims, legal/reputation risk, and it wrongly makes Sahas responsible for a vendor's product or promise.
- **Targeting individual reviewers from complaint pages** — rejected: privacy, platform-policy, and trust risk.
- **Unlimited "4 or 5" automations for ₹5,000** — rejected: unbounded scope destroys delivery quality and margins.
- **Demanding a platform migration or another subscription first** — rejected: contradicts the cost-saving promise. Recommend a change only after a documented technical blocker.
