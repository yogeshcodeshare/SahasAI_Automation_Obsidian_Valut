# Content Language Conventions

Sahas AI written content (notes, carousels, PDFs) comes in three languages. The full rules and fixed
label translations live in the skill's `references/languages.md` ([[notebook-notes-pdf-skill]]).

## Every language
Keep brand and tool names in English (WhatsApp, n8n, Meta, CRM, AI, API), along with numbers, ₹ amounts,
dates, and the agency name **Sahas AI Business Automation Agency**.

## Hinglish
- Roman-script Hindi sentences, with English tech words and English keywords.
- Address the reader as **aap**, with friendly imperatives (*karo, becho, check kar lena*).
- Spoken words like *poora funda, jhanjhat khatam, seedhi baat*. Avoid heavy Sanskrit Hindi.
- Labels: Tip: · Yaad rakho: · Catch: · Seedhi baat — · Swipe karo →

## Marathi
- Conversational Karad/Satara/Pune Marathi in Devanagari, addressing the reader as **तुम्ही**.
- English business words written the way they're spoken (क्लायंट, लीड, फॉलो-अप, बिझनेस); brand names stay in Latin letters.
- Full stop `.`, Western digits.
- Labels: टीप: · लक्षात ठेवा: · नोंद: · थोडक्यात — · धन्यवाद! · पुढे पाहा →
