# QR Review Code Pilot

## Experiment

Offer Manovedh a Google Review QR-code stand as a small, one-week pilot at a proposed ₹199. The purpose is to test whether a visible, easy-to-scan prompt helps genuine customers leave voluntary feedback. This is an experiment, not a proven price or recurring offer.

Link this pilot to the existing first-month delivery plan ([[manovedh-free-month-plan]]) and the review-hesitancy strategy ([[manovedh-review-hesitancy]]). If the workflow produces useful evidence, adapt it into the broader [[service-offerings]] catalogue and measure it using [[sahas-lead-automation-measurement-model]].

## One-week delivery SOP

1. Create a QR code that opens the clinic's official Google review flow and test it on more than one phone before delivery.
2. Place the stand where customers can see it after service, without obstructing staff or visitors.
3. Explain to the clinic team that the request must be voluntary, neutral and consistent—not limited only to people expected to leave positive feedback.
4. Ask genuine customers for an honest review without incentives, pressure, prepared wording or staff-written reviews.
5. Do not ask anyone to disclose treatment details publicly. Keep patient identity and health information out of the agency's notes and reporting.
6. Record only aggregate pilot results: start review count, QR scans if available, review-link visits if available, new reviews, and operational observations.
7. At the end of one week, compare the evidence with the baseline and decide whether to improve the stand, continue, or stop.

Suggested neutral request:

> If you have visited us, you may leave an honest Google review by scanning this code. Your feedback is optional and helps us improve.

## Success criteria

The pilot is worth repeating only if the QR is technically reliable, staff can use the request consistently, customers understand the action, and the measured result justifies the time and price. Do not treat a small one-week result as proof of long-term review growth.

## Reusable offer boundary

For later local-business sales, package the QR as a setup-and-design service with a tested destination, print-ready artwork and a short handover. Confirm the destination, ownership, editability and any hosting/redirect dependency before promising lifetime validity or unlimited scans. Pricing remains to be validated through pilots and sales conversations ([[pricing-ladder]]).
