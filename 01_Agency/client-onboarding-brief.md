# Client Onboarding Brief (reusable)

Step-1 capture for ANY new client before designing automation (from [[sop-agent-7-step-method]]). Fill one per client.

- Business name
- Website
- Industry
- Business type
- Location(s)
- Target audience
- Core offerings / services
- Lead sources
- Primary communication channels
- Client-reported pain points
- Current tools (if any)
- Reference files / links

> Do not paste GSTIN/PAN or personal identifiers into the vault copy — keep those in the client's own record. See [[gst-legal-structure]] for our own entity only.
