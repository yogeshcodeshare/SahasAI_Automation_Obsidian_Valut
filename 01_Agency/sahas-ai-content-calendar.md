# Sahas AI Content Calendar — 30 Days

**Planning window:** 14 September–13 October 2026 (India)
**Status:** Draft; short hooks and content outlines, not full scripts
**Publishing rhythm:** 3 short videos per week; 12 planned posts in the 30-day window

> The first three Sahas AI introduction videos are listed separately. They are not part of, and are not counted in, this 30-day calendar. Each hook is only an opening line, not a complete script.

## Separate pre-calendar: Sahas AI introduction videos

### Video 1 — Sahas AI कशासाठी सुरू केलं?

- **Hook (0–7s):** “Sahas AI म्हणजे नेमकं काय—आणि आम्ही कोणत्या विचारातून काम सुरू करत आहोत?”
- **Promise:** Viewers will understand Sahas AI’s purpose and the kind of practical business challenges it wants to work on.
- **Format:** Founder-led introduction to camera.
- **Why it’s better:** Establishes the agency’s purpose before discussing tools; include only founder details that have been confirmed.

### Video 2 — Sahas AI कोणत्या अडचणी सोडवू पाहतं?

- **Hook (0–7s):** “Enquiry, messages, follow-ups—हे सगळं लक्षात ठेवण्याची जबाबदारी नेहमी एका व्यक्तीवरच असावी का?”
- **Promise:** Introduces Sahas AI’s interest in clearer customer communication and follow-up processes, without claiming results.
- **Format:** Founder-led explanation with a simple text list.
- **Why it’s better:** Names the problem area honestly without implying a finished system or client success story.

### Video 3 — Automationमध्ये माणसाची भूमिका का महत्त्वाची आहे?

- **Hook (0–7s):** “Automation मदत करू शकतं—पण महत्त्वाचा निर्णय कोण घेणार?”
- **Promise:** Sets the expectation that people retain judgment, approval, and responsibility for complex situations.
- **Format:** Talking head with a simple person-and-process visual.
- **Why it’s better:** Introduces a human-first point of view instead of promising that technology can handle every situation.

## 30-day calendar

The calendar begins after the three introduction videos. WhatsApp templates are being prepared; complete workflows and AI-agent demonstrations are not presented as live capabilities.

### Week 1

#### 14 Sep — Month Post 1 — Automationची सुरुवात tool पासून नाही

- **Hook (0–7s):** “Automation सुरू करण्याआधी एक प्रश्न: नेमकं कोणतं काम पुन्हा पुन्हा होतं?”
- **Promise:** Help viewers identify a repeatable task before choosing a tool.
- **Format:** Founder to camera or text-led explanation with an everyday example.
- **Why it’s better:** Starts with the actual work and avoids treating a tool as the solution by itself.

#### 16 Sep — Month Post 2 — Class चालू असताना enquiry आली तर पुढे काय?

- **Hook (0–7s):** “Class सुरू आहे, मध्ये enquiry आली—आता पुढचं पाऊल कोणाच्या लक्षात राहतं?”
- **Promise:** Show why a clear next step matters when teaching and responding compete for attention.
- **Format:** Brief, clearly hypothetical coaching-class scenario; gentle humour is fine.
- **Why it’s better:** Makes the problem relatable without inventing a customer story or claiming lost sales.

#### 19 Sep — Month Post 3 — Enquiry आली की पुढची पायरी स्पष्ट आहे का?

- **Hook (0–7s):** “Enquiry आली—पण आता कोण respond करणार आणि पुढे काय होणार?”
- **Promise:** Explain the value of deciding who owns the next action.
- **Format:** Founder explanation with a simple text checklist.
- **Why it’s better:** Teaches a useful process principle without presenting an unbuilt workflow.

### Week 2

#### 21 Sep — Month Post 4 — WhatsApp हा संवादाचा मार्ग आहे, पूर्ण process नाही

- **Hook (0–7s):** “WhatsAppवर बोलणं म्हणजे संपूर्ण customer process तयार झाली असं नाही.”
- **Promise:** Clarify the difference between a communication channel and the steps that happen around it.
- **Format:** Simple channel-versus-process analogy; no API demonstration.
- **Why it’s better:** Helps viewers understand the business problem before discussing a technical product.

#### 23 Sep — Month Post 5 — Saved reply, message template आणि automation सारखे आहेत का?

- **Hook (0–7s):** “Saved reply म्हणजे automation का—की दोन्ही वेगळ्या गोष्टी आहेत?”
- **Promise:** Introduce the general difference between a prepared response and a process with multiple steps.
- **Format:** Three-label visual or founder explanation.
- **Why it’s better:** Clears up common terminology at a beginner level; verify current platform-specific definitions before publishing.

#### 26 Sep — Month Post 6 — Message लिहिण्याआधी तीन प्रश्न

- **Hook (0–7s):** “Message लिहिण्याआधी ठरवा—कोणासाठी, कशासाठी, आणि पुढे काय?”
- **Promise:** Give viewers a simple planning lens for making customer messages clear and relevant.
- **Format:** On-screen three-question list.
- **Why it’s better:** Connects to the founder’s current template-preparation stage without implying the library is finished.

### Week 3

#### 28 Sep — Month Post 7 — Follow-up helpful वाटतो की त्रासदायक?

- **Hook (0–7s):** “Follow-up helpful वाटतो की त्रासदायक—फरक कशामुळे पडतो?”
- **Promise:** Explore how relevance, clarity, and customer expectations affect the tone of a follow-up.
- **Format:** Founder explanation using a hypothetical example.
- **Why it’s better:** Discusses customer experience without scare tactics, performance numbers, or unverified policy claims.

#### 30 Sep — Month Post 8 — प्रत्येक situation automationकडे द्यावी का?

- **Hook (0–7s):** “Complaint आली तर पहिला response automationचा हवा की माणसाचा?”
- **Promise:** Help viewers recognize why complaints, unusual requests, and sensitive situations need human judgment.
- **Format:** Talking head with a few example categories; no technical handoff diagram.
- **Why it’s better:** Makes human involvement part of the design rather than claiming automation should handle everything.

#### 03 Oct — Month Post 9 — विस्कळीत माहितीवर automation केलं तर?

- **Hook (0–7s):** “Process automate करण्याआधी माहिती स्पष्ट आहे का, हे तपासलंय?”
- **Promise:** Explain why teams should agree on what information they need and where it belongs before automating steps.
- **Format:** Simple “messy notes” analogy or illustrative sketch.
- **Why it’s better:** Focuses on preparation instead of selling a CRM or promising a migration result.

### Week 4

#### 05 Oct — Month Post 10 — WhatsApp, n8n, AI agent आणि माणूस: वेगवेगळ्या भूमिका

- **Hook (0–7s):** “WhatsApp, n8n, AI agent—वेगवेगळं काम; मग माणसाची भूमिका काय?”
- **Promise:** Give a conceptual role map: customer conversation, app coordination, task assistance, and human approval or escalation.
- **Format:** Four-part text graphic; no live build or product demo.
- **Why it’s better:** Explains complementary roles instead of comparing the tools as replacements for one another.

#### 07 Oct — Month Post 11 — AI agent वापरण्याआधी कोणते प्रश्न विचारावेत?

- **Hook (0–7s):** “AI agentला कोणता निर्णय घेऊ द्यायचा—हे आधी ठरवलंय का?”
- **Promise:** Encourage viewers to define the task, boundaries, and point where a person should take over.
- **Format:** Question-led founder explanation.
- **Why it’s better:** Sets responsible expectations while keeping the topic conceptual; no claim that Sahas AI has a live agent.

#### 10 Oct — Month Post 12 — WhatsApp templates तयार करण्याचा माझा learning process

- **Hook (0–7s):** “WhatsApp template तयार करण्याचा प्रवास सुरू आहे—पण message आधी कोणते प्रश्न विचारायचे?”
- **Promise:** Share an honest learning reflection on purpose, recipient, and next step while preparing templates.
- **Format:** Founder reflection; show no private data or unfinished material that is not approved to share.
- **Why it’s better:** Builds trust through transparent progress rather than presenting a system as complete.

## Editorial safeguards

- The requested fields are provided for each item; hooks are opening lines only, not full scripts.
- Keep any further development at topic-and-outline level unless scripts or production details are requested separately.
- Do not invent customer stories, outcomes, metrics, credentials, or guarantees. Label hypothetical situations clearly.
- Treat competitor titles, quotations, figures, and technical explanations as research leads, not verified facts or wording to reuse.
- Verify current WhatsApp product, pricing, and policy details against official sources before publishing specifics.
- Describe n8n and AI-agent roles conceptually only; do not present them as completed Sahas AI services or working demonstrations.
- Treat coaching-class owners as an initial audience hypothesis to test, not a permanently validated segment.
