# Sahas AI — Agency Overview

**Sahas AI Business Automation Agency** — a solo AI-based business-automation + local-marketing agency for local SMBs (clinics, hotels, coaching classes, salons, shops) in Karad, Maharashtra (tier-3, Marathi-first).

- **Founder/operator:** Yogesh Mohite (currently salaried → runs the agency under his wife's proprietorship for privacy; see [[gst-legal-structure]]).
- **Legal owner:** Bhagyashri Yogesh Mohite (proprietorship).
- **Launch target:** 1 September 2026.
- **Expansion path:** Karad → Satara / Kolhapur → Pune / Mumbai.
- **Name chosen:** "Sahas AI" (साहस = courage/boldness). See [[brand-name-sahas-ai]].

## Operating philosophy (repeated, important)
**Ship first, optimize later. Do NOT collect tools before a paying client funds them.** Money-makers are decided: GMB Sarathi (built) + n8n (free) + WhatsApp via Veblika/GHL (bought) + Groq/DeepSeek (cheap). Everything else (video AI, Hermes, Paperclip) waits for a paying client or proven lead-gen. See [[ai-tools-stack-3-layer]].

- **"No client passwords, ever"** — client access via Manager invite or OAuth only. Applies to GMB and WhatsApp (client owns their WABA).
- Partner: **Bizautomation (Delhi)** mentors + provides reseller access (GHL/Veblika/VPS). See [[bizautomation-reseller-deal]].

Monthly burn pre-revenue ≈ ₹3,285/mo + one-time DataForSEO ₹5,000. One ₹2,999 client covers costs; two = profitable.
