# Decision — Car Dealerships First for the Initial Sahas AI Pilot

## Decision

Start the first niche-specific Sahas AI demonstration and pilot work with **car dealerships**, then evaluate med spas/skin clinics, GST/accounting firms, and home-service businesses.

## Context

Yogesh has an automobile-engineering background and roughly a decade of relevant teaching/professional exposure. That domain familiarity should reduce discovery risk while Sahas AI is still learning sales, scoping, delivery, and support. The first dealership demo has not yet been defined; the immediate work remains completion and testing of Sahas AI's own reusable WhatsApp, Veblika, n8n, and CRM foundations.

This decision changes the **first pilot priority** recorded in the older [[agency-go-to-market-sequence]], which previously treated coaching classes as the first scalable outreach vertical. Coaching classes remain a later candidate rather than being discarded.

## Alternatives rejected or deferred

- **Med spas / skin clinics first** — deferred because the business workflow and compliance context need more discovery.
- **GST/accounting firms first** — deferred until their repetitive workflows and service boundaries are better understood.
- **Home-service businesses first** — deferred until a narrower high-ticket subcategory is selected.
- **Real estate or marketing agencies first** — not prioritised because Yogesh sees these as crowded automation-pitch categories.
- **Coaching classes first** — moved later; the existing vertical research remains useful but is not the current first-pilot order.

## Depends on

- [[service-offerings]] — the underlying WhatsApp and workflow-automation services.
- [[veblika-native-chatbot-and-flow-roadmap]] — the reusable native WhatsApp foundations that must be proved first.
- [[whatsapp-automation-agency-phased-plan]] — the current stack sequence and integration gates.
- Assumption: Yogesh's automobile-domain familiarity will materially shorten dealership discovery and improve credibility.

## Consequences

- Do not build dealership-specific automations until the agency's own reusable stack is stable enough to demo.
- Before development, interview or observe dealership staff and map actual enquiry, test-drive, follow-up, finance/document, delivery, and service workflows. Do not assume a generic chatbot solves the highest-value problem.
- Keep the first pilot narrow, client-owned, measurable, and human-approved. The client pays required tool costs; free service work must have a written scope and must not be represented as recurring revenue.

## Review trigger

Revisit this decision if dealership discovery finds no urgent, measurable workflow that Sahas AI can safely deliver; if access to suitable pilot dealerships is poor; or if another niche produces a qualified paid opportunity first.
