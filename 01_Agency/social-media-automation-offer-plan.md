# Social Media Automation Offer Plan

## Positioning

Sahas AI can test a local-business social-content service inspired by the Jio-style Free/Growth/Scale structure. The offer should be framed as a managed content-and-enquiry system, not unlimited AI generation. Pricing and quotas remain experimental until the first three sales calls and delivery trials validate time, revisions and willingness to pay ([[pricing-ladder]]).

## Launch sequence

- **15 August:** internal/soft launch of Sahas AI's own social accounts and workflow.
- **15–31 August:** publish Sahas content, test approval/publishing, measure time per asset and collect enquiries.
- **1 September:** public paid launch only if permissions, quality and support are reliable.

## Pilot free tier

Proposed two-week pilot, subject to capacity testing:

- 14 single-image posts;
- 4 carousels, preferably weekend educational or offer content;
- business onboarding form;
- one content calendar;
- client approval before publishing;
- basic performance summary.

The pilot needs an explicit revision limit and end date. A free tier is an acquisition experiment, not a permanent promise.

## Growth experiment

The chat proposed testing an approximately ₹1,000/month inclusive-tax plan containing one daily image, eight monthly carousels, four AI videos and Instagram auto-reply support. This is **not a finalized price**. It may lose money if assets are manually produced, heavily revised or require paid video/API tools. Before publishing this price, record actual minutes, attempts, revisions, publishing effort, model/API cost and client response.

## Future Supercharge offer

A higher-touch Supercharge plan can later cover a client's presence across Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and LinkedIn. Introduce it only after the base workflow works. Charge for strategy, setup, editing, approvals, publishing, reporting and response management—not simply for AI-tool access.

## Account connection

Use official Meta permissions, OAuth or Manager/partner access where supported. Never request client passwords. Start with draft/export/approval mode, then enable publishing only after the client grants the correct role and the connection is tested. Instagram auto-replies remain gated until permissions, webhooks, rate limits, opt-outs and human handoff are verified ([[whatsapp-api-onboarding]]).

## Operating workflow

1. Social post or advertisement brings a prospect to WhatsApp.
2. WhatsApp captures the enquiry and sends the registration/intake form.
3. n8n creates or updates the lead record in the agency CRM/Sheet.
4. The form captures business type, audience, offers, service areas, languages, pain points, content preferences and approval contact.
5. Sahas AI prepares the content calendar and drafts.
6. Client approves, requests a limited revision, or rejects.
7. Approved assets are published through official integrations or exported for client posting.
8. Weekly reporting records output, reach, enquiries, response time and recommendations.
9. Upsell only from a diagnosed problem: lead follow-up, appointment reminders, review collection or WhatsApp qualification.

## Interviews and testimonials

An optional problem-discovery call can be offered after the pilot. Recording, storing or publishing the call requires clear consent and separate permission to use the material as marketing content. Collect a testimonial only after the client experiences the service; do not promise an extension in exchange for a positive review.

## Unit-economics checklist

Track per approved asset:

- generation attempts;
- human editing and copywriting minutes;
- revisions;
- publishing and approval time;
- model/API, storage and messaging cost;
- client-reported outcome.

If the ₹1,000 plan cannot be delivered within a controlled time budget, reduce the included quantity, raise the price, or keep it as a limited pilot.

## Initial stack

- Gemini Pro/Nano Banana and ChatGPT for manual creative validation;
- Canva Free or approved templates for exact text and brand finishing;
- Google Drive and Sheets for assets, calendar and approvals;
- n8n for client-critical workflows;
- Veblika for WhatsApp where its capabilities are enabled;
- Hermes only for Yogesh's personal back-office work ([[ai-tools-stack-3-layer]]).
