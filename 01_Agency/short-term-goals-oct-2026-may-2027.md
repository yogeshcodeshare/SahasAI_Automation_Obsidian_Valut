# Sahas AI Short-Term Goals — Oct 2026 to May 2027

## Why these goals exist

- **Long-term goal (Yogesh):** build a business and reach **₹100 Cr net worth by age 55** (2041). Yogesh is 40 in 2026.
- **Vehicle:** Sahas AI, an AI agency.
- **These goals cover the next 8 months (until 31 May 2027).**
- **Decision filter (from [[sahas-ai-founder-blueprint-2026-09-10]]):** *Will this directly help Sahas AI get paying customers?*

## Yogesh's plan (his words, summarised)

1. **October 2026:** finish all the demo automations by 31 October.
2. **November–December 2026:** sales, marketing and networking. Join the **Saturday Club** from November and network with business owners there to learn in depth how their businesses work.
3. **From January 2027** (earlier if November–December leave spare time): build **Claude AI employees, agentic AI and Claude skills**.
4. **Start one-to-one AI coaching first.** Offer **AI consulting to businesses** later, after more growth and understanding.
5. **Up to May 2027:** win as many business clients as possible.

### Services Sahas AI will sell

- **Business automation:** take repetitive tasks off employees so they can do more important work.
- **AI staffing:** provide AI employees.
- **Custom agentic AI:** built for each client's specific problem *(grow into this gradually)*.
- **AI coaching (1:1) → AI consulting (businesses)**.

> This plan updates the [[sahas-ai-founder-blueprint-2026-09-10]] eight-week sequence. Demo building ends on 31 Oct, and Nov–Dec are given mainly to sales and networking.

## Month-by-month goals, each split into two 15-day periods

Targets in the *Measure* lines were drafted by Claude as starting points. Revise them at each monthly review.

### October 2026: Finish all demos
**Monthly goal:** every demo automation completed, tested and ready to show.

| Period | Goal | Measure |
|---|---|---|
| **19–25 Oct** | List all demos, cut weak or duplicate ones, then finish the top demos (e.g. Renault WhatsApp agent, Email AI agent, Resume screener) | Final demo list; each demo published and tested |
| **26–31 Oct** | Record a 2–3 min walkthrough video of each demo, take proof screenshots, and add a demo section to SahasAI.in | 1 video + screenshots per demo; website updated |

*(October's first half, 1–15 Oct, has already passed. The rest of the month is split into two parts.)*

### November 2026: Sales, marketing & networking (start)
**Monthly goal:** launch the sales engine and join the Saturday Club.

| Period | Goal | Measure |
|---|---|---|
| **1–15 Nov** | Join the Saturday Club and attend the first meetings. Prepare a 30-second introduction, a one-page offer and a visiting card or QR code. Package the paid pilot offer. Start outreach. | Joined; 10 business owners met; 20 personalised outreach messages; pilot offer ready |
| **16–30 Nov** | Do discovery conversations to learn how businesses actually run, demo to interested owners, send proposals. Post 3 videos a week. | 5 discovery calls; 3 proposals; **first paid pilot** |

### December 2026: Sales, marketing & networking (convert)
**Monthly goal:** first paying clients and the first AI coaching students.

| Period | Goal | Measure |
|---|---|---|
| **1–15 Dec** | Deliver the first pilot. Keep networking every Saturday. Design the 1:1 AI coaching offer (topics, sessions, price). | Pilot delivered; coaching offer page or post ready |
| **16–31 Dec** | Get the first client results, ask for a testimonial or referral, and enrol the first 1–2 AI coaching students. Review the quarter. | 2 paying clients; 1–2 coaching students; review written |

### January 2027: AI employees & Claude skills (build)
**Monthly goal:** build the first AI employee that can be sold, while sales continue.

| Period | Goal | Measure |
|---|---|---|
| **1–15 Jan** | Learn and build: Claude AI employee setup and Claude skills. Choose 1 business role, e.g. receptionist, sales follow-up or back-office. | 1 working AI employee prototype |
| **16–31 Jan** | Turn it into an "AI Staffing" offer with a monthly price. Demo it to the Saturday Club contacts. Continue coaching. | AI Staffing offer live; 3 demos given |

### February 2027: AI staffing launch
**Monthly goal:** first AI staffing client, and convert automation clients to monthly retainers.

| Period | Goal | Measure |
|---|---|---|
| **1–15 Feb** | Pitch AI staffing to existing clients and leads. Convert pilot clients to monthly plans. | 1 retainer; 3 AI staffing proposals |
| **16–28 Feb** | Sign the first AI staffing client and onboard them with human-handoff safety. | **1 AI staffing client**; ₹ monthly recurring revenue tracked |

### March 2027: Agentic AI & scale clients
**Monthly goal:** first custom agentic AI project, and a steady flow of new clients.

| Period | Goal | Measure |
|---|---|---|
| **1–15 Mar** | Build a repeatable "problem → custom agent" discovery process (questions, scope template, pricing). | Discovery template + agentic AI proposal format |
| **16–31 Mar** | Win the first custom agentic AI project. Publish one case study, using only real and approved results. | 1 agentic project signed; 1 case study |

### April 2027: Systemise & grow
**Monthly goal:** more clients with less founder time per client.

| Period | Goal | Measure |
|---|---|---|
| **1–15 Apr** | Write SOPs for delivery, reuse demos and skills, and turn the best offer into a fixed package. | SOPs for top 2 services |
| **16–30 Apr** | Raise outreach volume through referrals, the Saturday Club and content. Use coaching to learn what people ask. | 5 new proposals; 2 new clients |

### May 2027: Review & next 6-month decision
**Monthly goal:** use evidence to pick the main service and industry for the next phase, and test whether consulting is ready.

| Period | Goal | Measure |
|---|---|---|
| **1–15 May** | Collect clients and income from all services (automation / AI staffing / agentic / coaching), and find which earns the most for the least effort. | Revenue per service |
| **16–31 May** | Decide whether to start AI consulting for businesses. Set goals for Jun–Dec 2027. Update this note. | New goal note written |

**Target by 31 May 2027 (draft, revise as needed):** 8–10 paying business clients, at least 5 on monthly retainers, 1+ AI staffing client, 1+ agentic AI project, and 5+ coaching students.

## How to run it (from the "How to Make / How to Achieve" mind maps)

- **Daily:** write a to-do list the night before. Do sales and outreach before building. Learn only what the current task needs. Don't spend more than 45 minutes stuck on one obstacle.
- **Every 15 days:** check that period's measures and move unfinished items forward or drop them.
- **Every month-end:** review the month and revise next month's goals.
- **Rewards/punishment:** reward a met 15-day goal (Yogesh chooses the reward). If the daily outreach is missed, no social-media scrolling that night.
- **Parked until reactivated:** Google Ads, GMB work, new business ideas, unrelated courses.
