# Sahas AI Logo Direction

## Approved direction to explore

- A premium, original connected mark suggesting four linked parts of a business system: owner, customer enquiry, automation, and growth.
- A clear **SAHAS AI** wordmark: approachable and readable, with `AI` receiving a restrained saffron/gold accent.
- Primary palette: deep ink/navy, warm off-white, and one muted saffron/gold accent.
- The mark must work as a favicon, WhatsApp/Instagram profile image, website header, PDF mark, and monochrome stamp.

## Guardrails

- Do not trace, reproduce, or closely imitate the four-loop mark from the supplied visual reference. It is inspiration for composition only; the final symbol must be recognisably original.
- Avoid generic robots, circuit boards, neon/cyberpunk styling, and overly thin lettering that fails at small sizes.
- Do not treat any AI-generated asset as final until Yogesh reviews it at small and large sizes, on light and dark backgrounds, and checks domain/trademark risk.

## Required delivery set

1. Horizontal full-colour logo.
2. Stacked logo.
3. Icon-only mark.
4. Light, dark, monochrome, and transparent variants.
5. SVG/vector source plus web PNG exports.
