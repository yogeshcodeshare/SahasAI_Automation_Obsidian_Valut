# Agency Go-to-Market Sequence

This is the ordering and decision-gate note for Sahas AI. It deliberately does **not** set prices, repeat service scope, or replace operational SOPs; those stay in the linked source notes.

## Phase 1 — establish a credible, ownable front door

Keep the public website accurate, complete WhatsApp/Meta onboarding with documentary details that match the legal proprietor, and confirm WABA ownership and exit rights. Do not make legal-registration, performance, or ranking claims that cannot be supported.

## Phase 2 — dogfood and prove delivery through a pilot

Run the agency's own lead process and deliver the Manovedh pilot with approve-before-publish and no-password controls. Treat a free pilot as learning and proof creation—not recurring revenue—until a paid agreement begins.

## Phase 3 — sell one focused vertical at a time

Use coaching classes as the first scalable outreach vertical. Lead with a useful audit/demo, diagnose the business bottleneck, and offer the smallest practical system that solves it. While DataForSEO remains intentionally deferred, use clearly labelled demo/synthetic audit material; do not promise live paid-data results or a Google ranking outcome.

## Phase 4 — validate the first three paid clients

Use the first three sales conversations to validate the emerging pricing ladder, service boundaries, onboarding effort, and message-cost pass-through. Keep the client responsible for their own WABA/number where possible, with Sahas AI as the setup and management partner.

## Phase 5 — scale only after repeatability is proven

Standardise onboarding and delivery using the agency's own funnel/SOP, then decide whether to remain on reseller tooling or operate an independent agency stack. The explicit review trigger is recorded in [[bizautomation-reseller-deal]].
