# Decision — Marathi Local-Business Lead Campaign Direction

## Decision

Use a pain-led, Marathi-first campaign direction for Sahas AI's future local-business acquisition: **"ओरडून नाही, system ने business वाढतो"**. The campaign must take a business owner from a relatable missed-enquiry moment into Sahas AI's WhatsApp qualification and booking flow.

The active delivery focus is **WhatsApp automation only**. Reel shooting scripts, Canva creatives/carousels, and social-media automation are explicitly deferred until the social-media workstream begins.

## Context

Local owners can be busy serving customers while WhatsApp, calls, Instagram, or Facebook enquiries receive no reply. The campaign story is not that the business is poor; it is that a good business can still lose a ready customer when there is no timely response or follow-up.

Suggested narrative anchors:

- "दुकान उघडं होतं… पण ग्राहकासाठी बंद होतं."
- "आज ग्राहक बाजारातही आहे आणि मोबाईलवरही आहे."
- "तुमचं काम चांगलं आहे. पण ग्राहकापर्यंत वेळेवर पोहोचणंही तितकंच महत्त्वाचं आहे."

The approved CTA concept is a prefilled Click-to-WhatsApp message asking Sahas AI for help managing enquiries, followed by a **10-minute Business Growth Check**. It is an offer to assess the enquiry process, not a promise of free automation or guaranteed business growth.

## Future WhatsApp Intake Direction

1. A Click-to-WhatsApp ad, website button, QR code, referral, or Google profile opens WhatsApp with a clear prefilled intent message.
2. The automated opener asks for business type and lets the lead choose a primary pain: missed enquiries, no follow-up, no booking process, or low Google visibility.
3. Capture business name, city, business type, approximate monthly enquiries, and the lead's stated problem in the existing Contact Master / intake design.
4. Apply source and pain tags, for example `SOURCE: Marathi Growth Reel`, `PAIN: Missed Enquiries`, and the existing pipeline/service-interest tags where applicable.
5. Send only a relevant demo or example, then offer the shared call-booking step from [[agency-automation-plan]].
6. After booking, send confirmation and 24-hour / 1-hour reminders. The Day 2 / 4 / 7 nurture sequence must stop on reply, booking, opt-out, or human handoff.
7. Any proactive message outside the WhatsApp customer-service window requires the correct approved template and an eligible, opted-in contact.

## Alternatives Rejected or Deferred

- **Generic AI-feature pitch** — rejected because local owners relate first to missed customers, delayed replies, and follow-up headache, not technical terms.
- **Copying MBG Card's characters, wording, or success claims** — rejected. The supplied videos are structural inspiration only; Sahas AI needs original situations, copy, and evidence.
- **Revenue guarantees, invented case studies, or "customers will run to you" claims** — rejected because they are not evidence-backed.
- **Cold/scraped-number broadcasts** — rejected because they conflict with consent, deliverability, and number-safety requirements.
- **Reel production, Canva carousels, and social-media automation now** — deferred. They belong in the later social-media automation workstream, not the current WhatsApp build.

## Guardrails

- Marathi is the primary local voice; Hindi and English can be offered as alternatives. Use a respectful local-business tone and never frame Marathi against another community or language.
- Keep deterministic capture, tagging, booking, reminder, and opt-out actions in Veblika/n8n. Use AI only for bounded, open-ended assistance with an available human handoff.
- Do not claim a response-time guarantee, uplift, lead volume, conversion result, or customer testimony until Sahas AI has verifiable evidence.
- Customer sends remain approval-led and subject to Meta/provider policy, template approval, and opt-in status.
