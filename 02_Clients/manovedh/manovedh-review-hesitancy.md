# Manovedh — Review Hesitancy Solution

## Problem (vertical-specific)
Mental-health patients hesitate to give public Google reviews (stigma — won't put their name on "cured my depression/addiction"). No testimonials created yet. A generic "review machine" breaks here.

## Solution
- **Reframe:** ask about the EXPERIENCE not the treatment ("तुमचा इथला अनुभव कसा होता?"). Their existing 30 reviews already do this (vague-positive, condition-free).
- **Identity-optional:** tell them first-name/initials only is fine — removes ~80% of hesitation.
- **Personal ask by Dr Sachin at peak gratitude** (end of course) beats an automated ask. Flip the ratio: mostly personal + QR/link, WhatsApp only as a soft nudge.
- **Yogesh = testimonial #1** (willing, not ashamed, now a business owner). Recruit 2–3 more willing "graduates" (face optional: silhouette / voice-only).
- **Authority proof substitutes for testimonials:** aggregate anonymous numbers ("2000+ लोकांना मदत", years of experience), Dr Sachin on camera explaining conditions/EFT. **For mental health, authority > testimonials.**
- **Hard lines:** NO incentivized reviews (Google bans, can wipe profile), NO fake reviews. Genuine + identity-optional only.

## Reusable pattern
For stigmatized/sensitive verticals: ask about experience not outcome, make identity optional, lead with authority content over patient testimonials, keep the ask personal.
