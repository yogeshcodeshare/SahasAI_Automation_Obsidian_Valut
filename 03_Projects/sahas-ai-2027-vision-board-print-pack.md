# Sahas AI 2027 Vision Board Print Pack

This note records the founder-confirmed content and production constraints for a physical vision board behind Yogesh's desk. It is a reusable brief, not evidence that the final printable Canva pack is complete.

## Physical format and production decision

- Final thermocol board: **100 cm wide x 50 cm high**, landscape (2:1).
- Main title: **SAHAS AI x LIFE VISION — 2027**.
- Build the board from editable **A4 portrait print pages**. Print, cut the individual strips/cards with scissors, arrange them on the board, and paste only after checking the full composition.
- Do not use a photo collage or photorealistic people. Use the Sahas AI logo, restrained line icons, thin borders, subtle geometric motifs, and coordinated colour accents.
- Keep long titles on one line. Leave most card area blank, with faint lines where useful, so Yogesh can add commitments in his own handwriting.
- Preferred visual direction: warm cream background, charcoal/brown text, muted Sahas gold, and restrained soft green accents. Follow the logo rules in [[sahas-ai-logo-direction]].

## Approved board structure

Use three vertical zones with visible breathing room:

1. **Left — Sahas AI Business Automation Agency**: Purpose & Mission; Ideal Clients; Core Services & Systems; Business Targets & Milestones.
2. **Centre — North Star / Main Goal**: Main Goal; Why This Matters; 2027 Milestone; Next 90 Days.
3. **Right — My Future Self**: Future Self; Health & Energy; Family & Relationships; Wealth & Freedom; Travel & Experiences; Personal Growth; Home & Lifestyle.

A practical composition is approximately 37 cm / 18 cm / 37 cm for the three zones, with the remaining width used for outer margins and gaps. Treat those column numbers as an assembly guide, not a precision cutting specification.
