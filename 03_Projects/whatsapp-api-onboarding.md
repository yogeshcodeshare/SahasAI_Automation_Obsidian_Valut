# WhatsApp API Onboarding

## Objective

Set up the agency's official WhatsApp API account through Veblika/Bizautomation. This will support agency messaging and, later, approved client automation workflows. Client-critical workflows belong in n8n/Veblika/GHL—not in an AI chat.

## Readiness package

- GST registration evidence for the legal proprietorship.
- Udyam/MSME certificate under matching proprietor/legal details, if required.
- Proprietor PAN and registered-address evidence only if the provider requests them.
- A live HTTPS website on the agency domain with business contact details, Privacy Policy, Terms, and the legal service-brand disclosure.
- Meta Business Portfolio details that match documentary legal name and address.
- Domain DNS access for Meta verification.

The selected official API number is intentionally not repeated in this vault note. Keep it able to receive OTPs and do not register/migrate it through a consumer WhatsApp app without written provider guidance.

## Non-negotiable ownership and safety checks

1. Confirm in writing whether the WABA and phone number are owned in the agency's own Meta Business Portfolio—not locked to Veblika or a reseller.
2. Confirm account/data migration, export, cancellation, and template ownership terms.
3. Confirm how Meta conversation/template charges are passed through.
4. Never request or store a client password. Use Manager access or OAuth where a platform supports it.
5. Keep approve-before-publish: AI may prepare a draft, but the agency/client must approve a customer-facing message before it is sent.

## Current dependencies

The public website and consistent legal/service-brand details are prerequisites. Formal trade-name or registration claims must not be made until confirmed. See [[sahas-ai-website]], [[brand-name-sahas-ai]], and [[whatsapp-automation-vendors]].
