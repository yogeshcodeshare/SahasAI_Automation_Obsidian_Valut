## Practical operating interpretation

- Verify the correct business, page, profile, and account before connecting or publishing. Multiple accounts make destination mistakes easy to miss.
- Use least-privilege access for people and partners. Publishing and replying are separate responsibilities from merely viewing content.
- Treat Media Storage as a library, not an approval system. Confirm asset rights, client approval, brand accuracy, and the intended channel before use.
- Review the caption, media crop, hashtags, links, CTA, channel-specific settings, and preview for every selected destination.
- Keep content in draft or approval state while copy, creative, timing, recurring settings, or follow-up comments are awaiting human review.
- Treat Post, scheduling, comment likes, and comment replies as external side effects that require owner approval.
- Read the full comment thread before responding. Escalate complaints, privacy requests, legal issues, sensitive topics, and promises of service to a human owner.
- The supplied course description mentions recurring content, CSV workflows, approval links, social listening, categories, watermarks, and media optimization. Those topics are retained in the supporting text but are not claimed here as visually verified configuration from this sparse frame pass.
