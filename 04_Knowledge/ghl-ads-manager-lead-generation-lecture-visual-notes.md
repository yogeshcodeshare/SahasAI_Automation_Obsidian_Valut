## Visually confirmed flow

1. **Media preparation:** GoHighLevel Media Storage shows folder tiles, search, modified-date sorting, filters, grid/list controls, Upload, Manage Canva, Connect Drive, and image/video assets.
2. **Meta business access:** Meta Business Suite shows business-portfolio switching, portfolio creation, ad-account settings, people/partner assignment, connected assets, and permission-related states.
3. **Campaign hierarchy:** Meta Ads Manager visibly separates Campaigns, Ad sets, and Ads. The campaign table includes delivery/status, results, cost per result, budget, amount spent, and impressions.
4. **Creative setup:** the GHL ad builder shows an ad-set tree, headline rows, description rows, a CTA selector, a multi-advertiser-ads option, Single/Carousel media choices, media upload, and Feed/Story/Reels preview tabs.
5. **Image editing:** the editor exposes Custom, 1:1 Square, 4:5 Portrait, and 9:16 Story crop presets, plus filters and Save/Cancel controls.
6. **Placement QA:** Advanced preview shows multiple Instagram and Facebook placements and surfaces an Unsupported aspect ratio warning for some placements.
7. **Lead form:** the Facebook form builder shows custom questions such as City and Interested In, Privacy policy link fields, Add custom disclaimer, and a Message for leads section with headline, description, CTA choice, CTA text, and link.
8. **Review state:** after form creation, the campaign returns to the builder and later appears in Meta Ads Manager with In review state, a Leads objective, budget strategy, recommendations, and a publish acknowledgement.

## Practical operating interpretation

- Treat business-portfolio, ad-account, people, partner, and connected-asset permissions as prerequisites. A permission warning is a governance gate to resolve with the account owner, not something to bypass.
- Keep the Campaign → Ad set → Ad distinction explicit when documenting a build. Objective and high-level settings belong at campaign level; audience, budget allocation, schedule, and placements belong at ad-set level; creative, form, CTA, and destination belong at ad level.
- Use the Media Storage library deliberately. Folder presence is not proof that an asset is approved, licensed, or correct for the current client.
- Review every headline, description, CTA, offer, price, comparison, and performance-related claim before publish. Screenshot copy is draft creative, not a verified claim.
- Use the built-in crop/editor tools only after preserving the approved source and checking brand colour, small-text legibility, and placement-safe areas.
- Treat Advanced preview as a required QA step. Check Feed, Story, Reels, mobile, desktop, and right-column variants; resolve unsupported aspect ratios or intentionally exclude the affected placement.
- Collect only the lead fields needed for the follow-up. Confirm the privacy policy URL, custom disclaimer, completion message, CTA, and destination before creating or publishing the form.
- Keep the campaign in draft/review until the owner approves the final creative, targeting, budget, data collection, follow-up, and publish action. Meta recommendations are not approval and do not guarantee lower cost or better performance.
