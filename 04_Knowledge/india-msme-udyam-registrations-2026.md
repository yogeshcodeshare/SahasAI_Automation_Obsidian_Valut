# India MSME Udyam Registrations (2026)

- **Total:** over **7.83 crore** enterprises were registered on the Udyam Registration Portal plus the Udyam Assist Platform as of **28 Feb 2026** (PIB).
- **March 2026:** reported at **7.9 crore+** across both platforms, split as ~4.72 crore on the Udyam Portal and ~3.21 crore on Udyam Assist.
- **Why it matters:** it's a rough ceiling on the formally registered small-business pool Sahas AI can sell to. It is not a count of businesses that would buy automation.

- **Caution:** the March split came via secondary reporting of the PIB figures. Re-check the latest PIB release before quoting it publicly.
