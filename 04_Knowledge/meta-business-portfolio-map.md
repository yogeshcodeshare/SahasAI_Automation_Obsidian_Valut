# Meta Business Portfolio — What Lives Where

`business.facebook.com` → Meta Business Suite → Settings. The trainer's claim: from this one window you manage **everything** about a portfolio — Facebook, Instagram, ads, WhatsApp.

A **business portfolio** is the container that owns pages, ad accounts, Instagram accounts, WhatsApp accounts, catalogs and payment settings. One per business; an agency accumulates one per client.

## The settings map

| Section | Contains | Why you'd go there |
|---|---|---|
| **Users → People** | Humans with access | Invite staff, set **temporary access with an expiry date** |
| **Users → Partners** | Accounts holding access | Your BSPs appear here |
| **Users → System users** | Programmatic access | — |
| **Accounts → Pages** | Facebook pages | Add existing or create new |
| **Accounts → Ad accounts** | Ad accounts | Create if running ads; catalogs feed ads |
| **Accounts → Instagram accounts** | IG business accounts | Claim or add |
| **Accounts → WhatsApp accounts** | Your WABAs | **This is the one that matters** |
| Data sources | Pixels, datasets | Ads only — **not** WhatsApp API. Don't configure here for WhatsApp |
| Billing & payments | Card, invoices | → [[whatsapp-api-billing-credit-line]] |

## Inside a WhatsApp account

Tabs: Summary · People · Partners · Phone numbers. The Summary carries:

- **Business verification** — should read Verified → [[meta-business-verification-notes]]
- **Account status** — should read Approved
- **Payment method** — names the BSP whose credit line bills you (the trainer's accounts show *Gupshup Technology India Pvt Ltd* and *ValueFirst Digital Media Pvt Ltd*)
- Currency, time zone, business name
- Two exits: **Payment settings** and **WhatsApp Manager**

If you ever need to know which provider actually sits between a number and Meta, the Payment method line is where it is written.

## Temporary access — the agency practice worth adopting

Invitations are by email; Meta then asks which assets and what level. You can set access to expire on a date, after which it lapses or you are prompted to extend.

**Why it matters:** you are being handed a client's ads, pages and WhatsApp account. Agreeing an expiry at the point of granting is cleaner for both sides than an open invitation nobody remembers to revoke — and it reads as professional. Relevant to [[whatsapp-api-onboarding]].

## Partners = your BSP history

The Partners list shows every provider with access. The trainer's shows Gupshup, DoubleTick, ValueFirst Digital Media and others — he tried many before settling on one that could deliver everything at the lowest budget. Useful market context for [[whatsapp-automation-vendors]].

## WhatsApp Manager

Reached from the WhatsApp account. Holds: Message templates (Manage, Template library) · Account tools (**Insights**, Messaging limits, Flows, Phone numbers, **Catalogue**, Activity log) · **Payment configurations**.

**Insights** is the screen to open when a client questions their bill — it shows the free/paid split and per-category charges in rupees, straight from Meta.

**Display name rejection** also surfaces here. The name must be supported by your certificate of incorporation or appear on your website. A rejected name does not stop messaging — status stays Connected — but it is what customers see. Get it right at onboarding using the registered business name.
