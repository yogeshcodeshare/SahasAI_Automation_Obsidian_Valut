# Social Media Automation Competitor Reference — Jio App and Interakt

## Evidence boundary

This note records a strategic analysis of the supplied Jio social-media automation video/transcript and the public Interakt pages reviewed alongside it. It is competitor/reference research, not verified product documentation. Exact plan limits, prices, quotas and feature availability must be rechecked before copying any claim into Sahas AI sales material.

## Jio-style product model

The video presents a productized social-media assistant: a business supplies its context, the system helps create content, and the user receives a repeatable path from idea to social publishing. The visible commercial pattern uses a free entry tier and higher paid tiers such as Growth and Scale. The useful lesson is the packaging structure, not the exact numbers.

The strongest lessons are:

- sell a recurring content outcome, not a list of AI tools;
- separate free acquisition, paid repeatability and higher-touch scale;
- make the workflow obvious: business intake → content plan → image/carousel/video generation → approval → publishing → reporting;
- expose platform/account permissions clearly;
- keep human approval before publishing;
- use usage limits so a low-priced plan cannot create unlimited manual work.

## Interakt-style reference

Interakt is a useful reference for a WhatsApp-first automation product: lead capture, templates, campaigns, automation flows, inbox/team handling and business follow-up are presented as a connected system. The design lesson is the simple path: social/website/WhatsApp entry → capture → qualify → follow up → human or business handoff.

Interakt is a product and positioning reference. Sahas AI should not copy its branding, interface or claims. The agency's WhatsApp stack remains Veblika + n8n + Google Sheets, with client-critical workflows in n8n rather than Hermes ([[ai-tools-stack-3-layer]]).

## Borrow

1. Productized recurring content plans.
2. A clear free-to-paid upgrade path.
3. One onboarding form for business context.
4. Approval and publishing status visible to the client.
5. A simple WhatsApp conversion path.
6. Reporting based on output, reach, enquiries and time saved.

## Do not promise yet

- fully automatic publishing to every social network;
- Instagram auto-replies without verified Meta permissions and tested API/webhook behaviour;
- unlimited assets at a low monthly price;
- guaranteed follower growth or lead volume;
- password-based account access;
- client-critical automation inside Hermes or browser macros.
