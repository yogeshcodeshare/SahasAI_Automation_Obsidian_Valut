## Practical operating interpretation

- Connect and verify the intended Instagram asset before enabling the workflow.
- Select the exact page, published post, and comment scope; decide whether first-level comments only is appropriate.
- Keep the public reply concise, helpful, and safe for a public thread.
- Use the DM step for the deeper explanation, qualification, or next action.
- Define the offer branch and timeout fallback in plain language; rename demo labels so operators understand the business rule.
- Verify the landing-page URL, tracking, mobile behaviour, and any eligibility or offer copy before activation. The exact URL and message copy are not visible in the recording.
- Test a matching comment, a non-matching comment, a nested reply, the public response, DM handoff, branch outcome, timeout, link, and human handoff.
- Monitor Social Planner > Comments after launch and retain a human review path for edge cases.
