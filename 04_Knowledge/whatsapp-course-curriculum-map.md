## Dependencies worth knowing

- **Lesson 4 assumes lesson 3.** The pipeline stages and custom fields must already exist; lesson 4 opens by asking you to bring them.
- **Lesson 4 depends on lesson 5.** Every broadcast demonstrated in lesson 4 sends a template, and a marketing template needs wallet balance — but templates themselves are not taught until lesson 5.
- **Lesson 8 expands the whiteboard** from lesson 4 → [[sop-automation-ai-progression]].
- **Lesson 10 returns to segmentation** using the tags and filters built in lesson 4 → [[whatsapp-tag-numbering-convention]].
