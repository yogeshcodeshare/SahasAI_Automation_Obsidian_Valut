# Grexa AI — Competitor Intel

Closest competitor to [[gmb-sarathi]]; also the funnel that sent our first client ([[manovedh-profile]]). Associated with Semrush's "GBP AI Agent". Claims 60,000+ / 3,60,000+ businesses.

- **Pricing:** ₹9,999/year (offer) or ₹15,000/quarter (list), no lock-in. Sells against "cost of a marketing person ₹18,994/mo".
- **Includes:** 120 keywords, unlimited AI review responses, ~30 reviews/mo, photo scheduling, performance analytics, WhatsApp AI agent, CRM.
- **11-step flow:** install → connect GBP → share details → AI keywords → profile improvement + automation setup → auto-reply reviews → schedule photos/videos → generate reviews → AI posts → track growth (before/after) → continuous improvement.
- **Results benchmark advertised:** Views +41.5%, Calls +108.7%, Directions +29.2%, +46% GBP interaction. **The bar to match/beat.**

## Our edge
Grexa is a self-serve English-first app with no human. Sahas AI offers **local, in-person, Marathi, done-for-you + a real person in Karad**, cheaper (target ₹5k/yr vs their ₹9,999/yr).

## ₹5k/year app vision
Yogesh wants to build a Grexa-style client-facing app (register → connect GBP → see points → AI actions log) on his own domain at ₹5,000/yr. GMB Sarathi is the engine; this is the v2 SaaS layer. Model the portal on Grexa's 11-step flow. Also researched competitor: Dhanda AI (₹11,999/yr, GMB Sarathi already 9/10 parity).
