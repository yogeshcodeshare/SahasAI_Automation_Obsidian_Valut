# Digital Akshay Webinar Funnel Reference

## Purpose

Private reference material supplied by Yogesh: a 13-message Marathi WhatsApp webinar sequence and eleven visual examples. It is preserved to study the campaign *structure*, not as Sahas AI copy or creative to reuse.

The screenshot assets are stored in [[06_Sources/Codex/2026-08-16-digital-akshay-workshop-reference]].

## Funnel pattern extracted

| Timing | Job of the message | Supplied example type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D-2 / D-1 | Announce date, time, topic and practical pains | workshop invite; pain-led education |
| Before session | Remove attendance friction | Zoom installation and connection check |
| 2 hours / 1 hour | Short reminder with one truthful reason to attend | time-based reminder visual and text |
| Live start | Send joining link once the session is genuinely live | live-now message |
| During live | Bring in late registrants without repeating every detail | late-join / final-call messages |
| After live | Give an approved replay or the next action | recording / bonus follow-up |
| Between events | Educate, poll interest, and warm the audience | problem education and group poll |

## What Sahas AI may reuse

- Marathi-first, plain-language explanation of a real business pain.
- One clear action per message: register, join, reply, or book a call.
- Calendar-friendly timing: invitation, reminder, live notice, and follow-up.
- A poll or reply keyword only when the response is connected to a real next step.

## Do not reuse

- The creator's name, portrait/likeness, business name, URLs, phone number, workshop title, or visual files in public Sahas material.
- Exact copy, unverified numerical claims, bonus valuations, revenue examples, claims about "thousands" of users, or false scarcity such as a replay being unavailable when it is available.
- "Secrets", "now or never", or other pressure language that cannot be substantiated.
