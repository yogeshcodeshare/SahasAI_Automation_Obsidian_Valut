# Training — Lesson 6 (WhatsApp Catalog & Payments)

Turns WhatsApp into a shopfront. Follows [[training-lesson-5-templates-mastery]]; position in the course at [[whatsapp-course-curriculum-map]].

## The structural insight

This is the lesson where the course **leaves the WhatsApp platform**. A catalog does not live in BizAutomation — it lives in Meta's Commerce Manager, attached to a Meta Business Portfolio, connected to your WhatsApp Business Account, and referenced from a template by two ID numbers.

Knowing which piece lives where is the difference between a working catalog and an afternoon of confusing errors. The trainer frames the general principle usefully: catalog and forms are **Meta features, not vendor features**, rolled out to different platforms' front ends at different speeds. The question to ask of any platform is not "does it have a button for this" but "can I reach this capability from somewhere".

## The commerce chain

Showcase the product → customer adds to cart → customer sends the order → payment detail goes to them → they pay. The trainer is candid that the last step is not available in every software, which is why payment configuration happens at Meta.

## Two findings worth acting on

**1. Never mislabel marketing as utility.** A catalog template must go in Marketing. Doing otherwise repeatedly teaches Meta's classifier the pattern, and your *genuine* utility templates begin being reclassified as marketing — capped and ~6× more expensive. The damage is gradual, invisible and hard to reverse.

**2. Shops are not available in India.** Stated on Meta's own Commerce Manager screen. You can still build a catalogue and use it for WhatsApp and ads, but there is no Facebook/Instagram storefront. Check this before promising a client one.

## Directly applicable to Sahas AI

The trainer's own catalogue lists **services, not goods** — Social Media Branding & Scheduling, Task & Team Automation System, Sales Automation Setup, Client Onboarding & Relationship Automation — each with a price and a link. The Sahas AI [[service-offerings]] could become a WhatsApp-sendable product list on the same pattern, with the Link field pointing at a payment form.

## On method

The trainer interrupts the technical content to make a study point: **implement alongside rather than watch and defer.** He estimates 25–30 days to cover the material at that pace, with the business live the following month. The failure mode he warns against is watching all twelve lessons then starting, by which point the early configuration is forgotten.
