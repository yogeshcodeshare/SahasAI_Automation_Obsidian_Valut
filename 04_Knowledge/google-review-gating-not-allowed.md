# No review gating when asking for Google reviews

- Several SEO sources report that Google's review policy forbids **selectively asking happy customers** for reviews
  ("discouraging negative reviews or selectively soliciting positive reviews"), and that Google may remove reviews when it finds gating.
- So a review flow must offer **the same Google review link to every customer**. It may *also* offer a private "had a problem" path —
  that is service recovery, not gating.
- Applied in [[whatsapp-review-feedback-agent]]; relevant to the GMB Sarathi offer too.
- Verify against Google's current "Prohibited and restricted content" page before quoting to a client.
