# WhatsApp Automation Vendors

## Veblika (chosen, via Bizautomation)
Real registered company — Brainvibs Technologies Pvt Ltd, Noida (care@veblika.com, +91 7599808080). Two sites: veblika.com (WhatsApp API + FB/IG automation + social scheduling) and veblika.in (broader all-in-one suite: HRMS/ATS/CRM/WhatsApp/workflow). Meta Verified, claims 500+ businesses. Capabilities relevant to us: WhatsApp Business API, drag-drop chatbot, broadcast/bulk, auto-responder, catalogue, custom workflows, API/webhooks, native Google Business + Sheets + IndiaMART integrations. No public pricing → the Bizautomation reseller route (~₹1k/mo) matters. See [[bizautomation-reseller-deal]].
**Confirm before onboarding clients:** whose WABA (client's Meta Business Manager, not Veblika's); 1 account = 1 client or multi; message-cost pass-through; data export.

## GHL (GoHighLevel) WhatsApp
Native LC WhatsApp (Meta Cloud API): threads in one inbox, WhatsApp in workflows, Conversation AI auto-answer, WhatsApp Flows (in-chat booking). Limits: 24-hr window (then paid templates), only Marketing + Utility templates. Best when the client needs a whole CRM.

## DoubleTick
WhatsApp-native: team inbox + agent seats, catalogue/order bots, campaigns + analytics. From ₹3,000/mo **annual-only** (+18% GST) → do NOT take annual-only billing risk on your balance sheet.

## Meta message costs (India)
Marketing ₹1.09, Utility ₹0.145, Service free. Pass to client.

## Model that makes churn free
Client owns their WABA + number in THEIR Meta Business Manager; you're admin; they pay BSP/Meta directly; you charge setup + management → zero stranded cost, no ToS risk. Matches the "no client passwords" rule ([[sahas-ai-overview]]).
