# Training — Lesson 3 (WhatsApp SOP & CRM Logic)

Completed 2 Aug 2026. Key takeaways (foundation session).

> These are a trainer's recommendations, not practices Sahas AI has yet validated in delivery.
> Kept at `supported` deliberately — promote individual claims only once they've worked on a
> real client.

- **Sales lead automation is the primary use case**; WhatsApp is the lead data-collection layer (capture details, requirements, intent/interest).
- **Lead stages:** New → Requirement Captured → Interested → Site Visit → Closed → Lost, plus a **Review/Reputation** stage. Track lost reasons. → [[lead-stages-pipeline]]
- **Site-visit sub-statuses:** Scheduled / Confirmed / Rescheduled / Cancelled (custom fields + sub-parameters).
- **WhatsApp as CRM:** standalone OR integrated with a 3rd-party CRM (backend DB + workflow).
- **Maturity ladder:** Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced. Day-1 beginner scope = Forms, Tagging, Gallery/documents, base workflow.
- **CRM setup:** Contact Master + Lead Stages + Custom Fields; "system-first selling".
- **Real-estate config:** project fields, property categories, Campaign ID, visit date; budget bands drive conversion triggers; dropdowns vs text by data type; state-wise selection.

Industry examples cited: travel agents, manufacturing/goods, real estate. Next lessons go deeper into workflow automation.
